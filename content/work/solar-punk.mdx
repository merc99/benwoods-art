Evershift is a solar-punk universe where humanity has unlocked immortality, not through conquest but through harmony with
the forgotten energies of time. With death no longer setting the pace, purpose has changed: explorers, scholars and rogue
technomancers search ancient lands and fractured timelines for relics that fuse ancient wisdom with future tech.

## Concept

A long-form comic unfolding over years, told through character sheets, relic blueprints, cryptic schematics, creatures
and illustrated timelines. The relics hold power and act as keys to hidden epochs.

## Development

Each illustration starts with "what if?" and moves through moodboards, character studies and environmental storytelling.
The aim is a consistent visual language that carries the tone, history and emotional arc of the world, with every
detail layered to reward a closer look.

## Execution

Concepts become finished pages: characters, relics and timelines rendered with deliberate color, texture and
composition.
