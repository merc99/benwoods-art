The Story of Prince Ham Doe is an illustrated narrative inside WAGDIE (We Are All Going to Die), a large, community-driven
roleplaying campaign in the spirit of dark fantasy and collaborative storytelling. It follows the fall of a once-noble
heir turned solemn wanderer, through grief, betrayal and spiritual reckoning.

## Concept

Ham Doe began as a side character and became a central figure as players connected with him. His arc draws on medieval
folklore, cathedral architecture and tarot archetypes. He isn't a traditional hero but a vessel of inherited guilt,
mirroring WAGDIE's core theme: that empires, identities and gods all must one day die, and maybe be reborn.

## Development

The story was built through collaborative improvisation, reflecting player choices and world events as they happened.
The art direction favors mood over realism: dark linework, aged textures and subdued palettes, like illuminated
manuscripts gone rotten.

## Execution

Comic-style compositions with theatrical lighting and symbolic framing, released alongside the campaign and shaped by
the community around it.
