MMO Nodes ("Stake. Level. Earn.") is a crypto game concept. Players mint characters across eight classes, stake them in
dungeons to earn $MMO, level and gear them up, and burn old items and tokens in the Volcano.

The prototype is a full clickable product: landing and lore, dashboard, character and inventory, mint with odds and
fusion, quests, PvP and clan wars, raids, the realm map, duels, a marketplace, alchemy, rewards, stats, a token page and
a guided first-run tour. It saves progress in the browser and offers a fresh start or a demo account.

The economy simulator runs the $MMO economy forward two years, week by week. Set emissions, player behavior and sinks,
then see whether burns catch up with emissions and what that does to price, supply, players and APR.

## Status

An interactive prototype with no backend or chain. It started as a response to a job posting and is now being
developed as a real product: tokenomics, mint and burn mechanics, and an integration with a trading app.
