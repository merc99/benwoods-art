The Retro-Futurism series extends the Evershift universe, merging mid-century sci-fi with solar-punk mythology. Each
piece explores otherworldly schematics, creatures and environments that blend ancient relics with imagined future
technology, and invites viewers to decode the lore through blueprints and animal studies.

## Concept

A multiverse where analog meets astral, inspired by vintage technical drawings, cosmic zoology and post-human design.
Each artwork is a window into timelines where civilization runs on light, memory and lost knowledge.

## Development

Each illustration starts with worldbuilding: mapping timelines, species and architecture within Evershift. Sketches move
from pencil to high-resolution digital work in Illustrator, Photoshop and TouchDesigner, with palettes, textures and
typographic glyphs kept to one retro-tech tone across the series.

## Execution

Finished as print-ready illustrations, often annotated with fictional metadata that translates the universe's logic
into diagrams.
