Dreamline is a lifestyle and clothing brand rooted in the power of dreams, shared inspiration and creative unity. It's
more than apparel: a platform where people co-create culture through playlists, journal entries and idea submissions.

## Concept

At the heart of the brand is a symbol of unity: the Star, the Eye and the Globe. The star is thought inception (source),
the eye is releasing resistance (self) and the globe is embracing the solution (community). Together they form a
narrative system, a cycle from idea to acceptance to shared reality.

## Development

The brand grew out of late-night conversations and sketchbooks full of symbols. It was built bottom-up, shaped by the
ideas of its supporters, through visual language studies and symbol experiments until the star-eye-globe iconography
became the anchor for every piece of content and merchandise.

## Execution

Every product and activation ties back to the cycle: inception, self-alignment, shared action. The merch, content and
digital presence work as connective tissue for a growing group of creators.
