Mamie's Sunday Best is a nostalgic toffee brand born from a family recipe passed down through generations in Kansas.
Made in small batches, with buttery richness, nutty crunch and a touch of chocolate, it's made to be shared like
a Sunday gathering.

## Concept

The brand is built around the idea of Sunday bests, the moments when everything feels just right. It draws on Midwestern
heritage and childhood memories of family, pets, homemade sweets and kitchen-table laughter. The Bichon Frisé mascot
stands for joy, love and that extra bit of sweetness.

## Development

The work started with keeping a family recipe alive and grew into a full brand. The visual system takes its cues from
vintage food labels and nature's palette: sunny yellows, soft browns and pops of blue. Illustrated chocolate bars, butter
cubes and flowers tie the product to its homemade roots, and the type is bold and welcoming.

## Execution

The identity comes to life through the illustrated ingredients and the Bichon, which appears across every product and
touchpoint.
