100K Club is a paid sports betting community on Discord with an earned rank ladder: Member, Rising Sharp, Sharp,
Featured Sharp and Club Legend. It's community-first. Members rise by posting and tracking their own plays, not by
following a few senior cappers.

Slipboard is the custom Discord bot behind it. A member posts a bet slip screenshot and tags @Slipboard; the bot reads
the image, grades it automatically from live scores, keeps the leaderboard and assigns rank roles.

The landing page sells the club and shows the ladder, a preview of the Discord, the leaderboard and recent wins. Its
hero crest tilts toward the cursor with a holo glare and a gold sweep clipped to the crest's shape, over a WebGL silk
shader.

## Status

Pre-launch. The landing page is live. Slipboard runs on Railway with Postgres and has auto-graded real slips. The
server isn't open to members yet.
