A paid sports betting club on Discord with an earned rank ladder, from Member to Club Legend. Members rise by tracking their own plays.

Slipboard is the bot behind it, fully custom and built for the club, then deployed to its Discord server. Members post a slip screenshot and Slipboard captures it, grades the bet and points them to the books with the best lines. Its built-in rank system tracks every result and promotes members automatically as they climb the ladder.
