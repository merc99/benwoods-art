A home and small-team security concept. An AI watches every device on your network, explains what it found in plain language, shows the exact evidence behind each flag, and only fixes things once you've reviewed them.

The prototype is a marketing site and a working dashboard in one self-contained page, with no framework and no build step. It covers light and dark modes, phones down to 375px, the keyboard and reduced motion.
