Check-in is a social habit tracker for small crews of 3 to 15 people. You join a crew, check in once a day with a short
note and a couple of tags, and see who else showed up. The line is "No shame days, just show up": it's an
accountability app, not a productivity app. Missed days aren't punished, streaks are secondary, and the all-time
check-in count never goes down.

It started in a group chat where friends posted what they did each day ("Chest day ✅", "Cold plunge"). Every message
was a small promise to show up. Check-in keeps that energy and gives it a home.

## Status

A portfolio build. It runs on built-in demo data that re-seeds on every load, relative to now, so the feed always
looks like today, and crewmates' activity is simulated (they check in and react every 9 to 16 seconds). A full Supabase
backend with email sign-in, schema and migrations exists on its own branch and isn't merged yet. A React Native version
lives separately and is behind the web app.
