The Gilded Can is a tinned-fish tapas restaurant concept that takes the best tins from around the world (anchovies,
sardines, mackerel) and serves them as a fine-dining experience that's bold, nostalgic and a little rebellious.

## Concept

It started with a question: what if guests treated a tin of sardines like caviar? Each
can is served with intention, alongside small plates and drinks inspired by Iberian coastal bars.

## Development

The identity blends utilitarian textures with curated minimalism. The sword-inspired logo is a nod to precision, to
slicing into something simple and finding it exquisite. Vintage ephemera and line illustration carry the old-world
side; the restraint carries the modern one.

## Execution

The website keeps the same simple, clean feel. The sword motif and lo-fi digital overlays carry
through the menus, signage and merchandise.
