FLEXIBLE is an illustrated, modular web3 project built for self-expression. Each character is a customizable
identity made from interchangeable traits, so it can change with its holder instead of staying a static profile picture.

## Concept

At its core FLEXIBLE is about tokenized expression: characters as avatars of participation in a community of designers,
developers and traders.

## Development

It began as a personal illustration experiment questioning static PFPs. Over months of sketching, digitizing and styling,
a full set of modular traits was drawn, layered and kept compatible across every trait category. A modular
contract architecture was designed alongside it so traits could be swapped and updated.

## Execution

The launch site pairs a minting interface with a live character preview and a connected wallet. Details of the release
are under NDA.
