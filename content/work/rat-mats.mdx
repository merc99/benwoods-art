Rat Mats is a premium desk mat brand I'm co-founding, built on gig-poster culture. Each series is one artist's artwork,
printed once in an edition of 500, every mat numbered on a sewn tag. When the numbers run out, the series is retired to
the archive.

The storefront is built around The Sheet: a 500-square grid per series showing which numbers are claimed, with 12 marked squares
that come with something extra (a mat from the next series, a signed artist proof, a year of Rat Club or a discount).
The marked squares are visible on purpose, so picking one is a choice, not a gamble. Type any number into the Registry
and its square flashes in the sheet above.

## Status

The storefront is live, pre-launch. Series 001 "Dart" is my artwork. Series 002, its artist, the claim counts
and the archive entries are placeholder data for design review.
