What raises your passive yield

Your dungeon APR is the base rate of the tier plus every boost you've earned. Here's an example stack and how to get each piece.

Example total10.3% APR
  • Dungeon tier4% to 8% base APRStake in higher tiers
  • GearUp to +1% from your main's gear score, plus set bonusesEquip better gear on your main
  • Compound bonusGrows each time you compoundCompound instead of claiming
  • Prestige+0.25% per prestige, up to +1.5%Reach Rank 30 and prestige
  • CouncilUp to +1% from vote power, plus gauge weights of 0.7× to 1.4×Lock $MMO and vote each week
  • Eruption+1% for 24 hoursBurn in the Volcano before it erupts

How gear fits in. Gear does two jobs. It raises your Power, which drives raid damage, your share of raid rewards and your guild war strength. And your main's gear score adds up to +1% APR on every dungeon, so better gear means a higher passive yield.

Good to know

What's the difference between claim and compound?

Claim sends pending rewards to your wallet, once every 24 hours per dungeon. Compound adds them to your locked stake, which raises future earnings and adds a bonus to that dungeon's APR.

Does rarity make a character stronger?

No. Rarity adds cosmetic traits, glows and auras. Gear is what changes a character's power and attributes.

What is Renown?

Renown is earned from everything you do. It raises your account rank and fills the season pass, which pays Embers, loot, gear, titles and a Relic.

What makes $MMO scarce?

Half of every summon is burned, claims carry a 5% tax (half burned), and anything thrown into the Volcano is burned forever. Emissions shrink 1% every week, and throttle down automatically when burns fall behind.

Can I link my fomo account?

Yes. Link it by proving you control the wallet behind your handle. You get a verified trader badge, clan standings and your fomo $MMO balance in the game.

How do guild wars work?

Stake characters with a guild. The side with more power when the timer ends wins rewards like badges, XP boosts and whitelist spots.

                    GAUGE VOTE

                    Split your vote across the gauges. Popular dungeons earn up to 1.4× their base APR next epoch; neglected ones drop to 0.7×. Incentives are posted by clans and guilds, and paid to that gauge's voters.

                    HOW THE COUNCIL WORKSRules
                    • LockLock $MMO for 1 to 52 weeks. It can't be withdrawn early.
                    • PowerVote power is your locked amount × weeks left ÷ 52, so it shrinks as the unlock date nears.
                    • BoostVote power adds up to +1% APR on every dungeon, reaching the cap when it matches your total stake.
                    • GaugesEach weekly epoch, votes set how emissions split across the four dungeons and the raid pool.
                    • FeesVoters share the epoch's protocol fees, pro rata by vote power.
                    • IncentivesAnyone can post $MMO to attract votes to a gauge. It's paid to that gauge's voters.
